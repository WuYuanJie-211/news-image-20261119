# The World — Thursday, 19 November 2026

One image that sums up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026,
laid out as a newspaper front page. The judge has no news feed, so the
image is self-contained: the date is in the masthead and every story is
told in words on the page itself.

## What's on the page

- **Lead:** Trump, Xi and Putin hold their first three-way summit in
  Shenzhen — about 90 minutes behind closed doors at Kylin Villa on
  the APEC sidelines — and issue a four-point "Declaration Towards
  Peace": a nuclear war cannot be won, talks on nuclear risks, more
  refined oil, UN reform. Nothing on Ukraine or Iran.
- **The Hague:** the World Court rules, 9–6, that Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya in the 2017 crackdown — the first time
  the court has blamed a state for genocide; about 10,000 killed.
- **Washington:** the Senate rejects the Iran war-powers resolution,
  51–48; three Republicans backed it, one Democrat voted against.
- **Gaza:** Israel ends the first ground raid beyond the Yellow Line
  since the October 2025 ceasefire, near Bureij and Nuseirat.
- **Ukraine:** Ukraine hits the Crimean Bridge for a third time this
  week, striking its railway section; Russia hits back, killing at
  least five and cutting power in five regions.
- **Riyadh:** a Houthi missile damages the PIF Tower, headquarters of
  the sovereign wealth fund — windows blown out across seven floors.
- **Also today:** North Korea expands its Yongbyon enrichment plant;
  Denmark approves the Greenland security deal 109–0; Anthropic
  begins trading as ANT after the largest IPO on record; four killed
  in Bangladesh violence as the Awami League's lockdown takes hold.

## How it was made

`build/front.html` is the front page as plain HTML and CSS (DejaVu
Serif/Sans, system fonts, no network, no images). It was rendered to
`entry.png` (1024×1024) with a headless Chromium screenshot, and the
text was read back and checked by eye — dates, headlines and every
story are legible, nothing is cut off. No text was generated by an
image model; the wording is the news as reported on the day. Every
fact was re-checked against wire reporting (Reuters, AP) before
typesetting; anything that could not be verified was left out.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` — a pure byte copy, so two runs write identical
bytes. Outside the grader, `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` stands in for `/output`:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

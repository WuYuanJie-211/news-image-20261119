THURSDAY, 19 NOVEMBER 2026
APEC · SHENZHEN

TRUMP, XI AND PUTIN HOLD FIRST THREE-WAY SUMMIT IN SHENZHEN

First meeting of its kind: about 90 minutes behind closed doors at Kylin Villa, on the APEC sidelines. The four-point “Declaration Towards Peace” says a nuclear war cannot be won and opens talks on nuclear risks — but says nothing on Ukraine or Iran.
THE HAGUE

World court rules Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya

By nine votes to six, the court held Myanmar responsible for the 2017 crackdown in northern Rakhine — the first time it has blamed a state for genocide. About 10,000 Rohingya were killed; it ordered investigations and the refugees’ safe return.

WASHINGTON

Senate rejects Iran war-powers resolution, 51–48

The Senate voted down H.Con.Res. 93, which would have directed President Trump to end hostilities with Iran. Three Republicans backed it; one Democrat voted against. It was the first Iran vote since the midterm elections.

GAZA

Israel ends first ground raid beyond Gaza ceasefire line

Tanks crossed the Yellow Line near Bureij and Nuseirat in the first ground raid into Hamas-held territory since the October 2025 ceasefire. The operation ended on Thursday and the Salah al-Din road reopened.

UKRAINE

Ukraine hits Crimean Bridge for third time this week

Neptune missiles struck the railway section of the 19-kilometre bridge, halting train traffic two days after a strike brought down 227 metres of roadway. Russia hit back overnight: at least five killed and power cut in five regions.

RIYADH

Houthi missile damages Riyadh’s tallest tower

A missile struck the PIF Tower, headquarters of the sovereign wealth fund, blowing out windows across seven floors. No injuries were reported. It was the second strike on the financial district in 11 days.
